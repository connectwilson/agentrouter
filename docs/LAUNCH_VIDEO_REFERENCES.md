# AgentRouter Launch Video References on X

Research snapshot: 2026-08-22. X engagement numbers change over time and should be treated as approximate snapshots, not audited campaign analytics.

## Shortlist

### 1. Cognition — Introducing Devin

- [Watch the original X post](https://x.com/cognition/status/1767548763134964000)
- Published: 2024-03-12
- Runtime: 1:49
- Snapshot: approximately 31.5M views, 42.3K likes, 9.6K reposts, and 12.4K quotes
- Best reference for AgentRouter: **yes — primary structural reference**

Why it worked:

- Opens with a founder stating a category-sized claim, then immediately proves it.
- Uses one understandable task rather than a feature montage.
- Shows the actual working environment: prompt, planning, browser, shell, code, debugging, and final result.
- Ends with a quantitative benchmark that gives the viewer something concrete to repeat.
- The UI is the spectacle. Motion design supports the proof instead of replacing it.

Borrow for AgentRouter:

- One end-to-end task as the narrative spine.
- Move from claim to real product within the first 10–12 seconds.
- Show the hidden system steps as a readable progression.
- End the proof sequence with one memorable quantitative or verifiable artifact.

Do not copy:

- The phrase “first” unless it can be defended precisely.
- A nearly two-minute runtime for the primary social post unless the demo remains visually active throughout.

## Recommended Reference Mix for AgentRouter

AgentRouter should not imitate one film wholesale. The strongest combination is:

- **Devin:** overall narrative and real UI proof
- **Figure 01:** continuity and trust — one request, one unbroken evidence chain
- **GPT-4o:** human-readable task and honest live-demo feeling
- **Sora:** first-frame hook and short teaser discipline
- **Sonos:** muted 15-second X cut and distribution packaging

In practical terms:

1. Open like Sora: show a surprising verified answer before the logo.
2. Structure like Devin: follow one task through the complete system.
3. Prove like Figure: preserve the same request and identifiers across every step.
4. Humanize like GPT-4o: start from a question a person would genuinely ask.
5. Cut for X like Sonos: ship a dedicated 15-second caption-first version.

## Revised Recommendation

The existing 70-second plan should make one change after this research: move a preview of the verified result into the first three seconds, then rewind to show how AgentRouter produced it. This gives the film a stronger X-native hook without sacrificing the full route/payment/evidence story.

Suggested opening:

> **0:00–0:03** — “BTC liquidation max pain: [actual captured value]” appears with `Verified · Paid · Evidence anchored`.
>
> **0:03–0:06** — The result collapses back into the user's original question.
>
> **0:06 onward** — “Here is how an agent found, paid for, and verified that answer.”

This opening is closer to the common pattern behind the strongest X launch posts: show the new capability first, then earn the explanation.
